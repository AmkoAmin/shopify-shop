=== PROMPT 1: RANKE (Stickerei, ca. 20 x 5-6 cm) ===
Bilder hochladen: (1) RBiH-Flagge, (2) mockup-longsleeve-v1.png

Create a flat embroidery production artwork, front view, on a fully transparent background. Recreate the lily vine from the "DETAIL – VORDERSEITE" close-up in image 2 as clean flat artwork: a horizontal flowing stem with about 8 to 10 leaves, one open natural lily blossom and one bud along the stem, ending on the right with a heraldic lily that copies the slender golden lilies in image 1 exactly (slender and tall, narrow pointed central petal, small curled side petals, thin band, slightly elongated foot).
Requirements:
- One solid color only: cream white #F2EBDD. No shading, no gradients, no thread texture, no fabric, no shadows.
- Designed for embroidery at 20 cm wide x 5-6 cm high: stems at least 2 mm thick at that size, every leaf at least 6 mm long, clear gaps of at least 1.5 mm between all elements, no hairlines.
- No stamens or tiny details inside the blossom; show the petals as simple solid shapes with small gaps between them.
- The heraldic lily must be about 4 cm high at final size.
- Wide horizontal format, aspect ratio about 4:1, high resolution.

=== PROMPT 2: LILIE ALLEIN (Stickerei, ca. 4 cm hoch, fuer "Ljiljan" und Beanie) ===
Bild hochladen: (1) RBiH-Flagge

Create a flat embroidery production artwork on a fully transparent background: one single heraldic lily that copies the slender golden lilies in image 1 exactly (slender and tall, narrow pointed central petal, small curled side petals, thin horizontal band, slightly elongated foot with small curled ends). Ignore the flag's colors, shield and stripe.
Requirements:
- One solid color only: cream white #F2EBDD. Filled solid shape, no outlines-only, no shading, no gradients, no texture.
- Designed for embroidery at 4 cm high: the thinnest part (band, curled ends) must still be at least 1.5 mm at that size, gaps at least 1 mm. Simplify tiny curls if needed, but keep the slender proportions.
- Centered, square format, high resolution.

=== PROMPT 3: RUECKENSCHRIFT (Druck, ca. 10-12 x 3 cm) ===
Bild hochladen: (1) RBiH-Flagge
Tipp: Den Text lieber selbst in Canva oder Figma setzen (z. B. Cormorant Garamond) und nur das Ornament per KI erstellen. KI schreibt Text oft fehlerhaft.

Create a flat print production artwork on a fully transparent background. Two lines of centered text in an elegant classic serif font, all lowercase, spelled exactly: "you met me at a very" / "bosnian time in my life". Below the text, a thin horizontal ornamental line with a tiny heraldic lily in the center that copies the slender golden lilies in image 1.
Requirements:
- One solid color only: cream white #F2EBDD. No shading, no texture, no background, no fabric.
- Designed for print at 10-12 cm wide and about 3 cm high: thinnest lines at least 0.5 mm at that size.
- Horizontal format, aspect ratio about 4:1, high resolution.
